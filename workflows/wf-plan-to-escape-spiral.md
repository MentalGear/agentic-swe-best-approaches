# Switch to planning when it spirals

rglover: "If it's in Act and starting to spiral (evidence of looking for something or trying to better
understand something), I'll kick into Plan and tell it to think a bit harder — also avoids it wasting
tokens retrying the same techniques" ([49852420](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49852420)).

thot_experiment sees the opposite default: without plan mode, "hours of time chasing its tail or
implementing kludges on kludges" where a simpler fix existed elsewhere
([49854035](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49854035)).

## Signals

Same file edited three times for the same error · tests toggling between two failures · growing
workarounds · "let me try another approach" repeatedly.

## Prompt

"Stop. Don't edit. Summarise what you've tried and why each failed. List three hypotheses for the
root cause, what evidence would distinguish them, and the cheapest check for each."
