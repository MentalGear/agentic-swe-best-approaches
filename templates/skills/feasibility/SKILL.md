---
name: feasibility
description: Quick feasibility check and solution options for an idea, without a detailed plan. Use when the user asks "is this feasible", "how would you approach", or wants options before committing.
---

Don't edit files. Don't write a detailed plan.

Read what you need, then reply with:

- **Verdict:** feasible / feasible with caveats / not feasible — one sentence why.
- **Options:** two or three approaches, each with effort (S/M/L), main risk, and what it rules out later.
- **Recommendation:** which one and why, in two sentences.
- **Unknowns:** what you'd need to check (and the cheapest way to check it) before committing.

Keep the whole reply under a screen.
