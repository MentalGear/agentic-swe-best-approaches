# Cargo-cult mega-prompts

Nursie: "I'm not convinced that 'You are an experienced senior software engineer and an expert
reviewer, here are five pages of instructions on how to do a review' style prompts are massively
useful any more" ([49852894](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49852894)). tetrisgm: "Much of what
distinguished a knowledgeable AI dev a year ago is now an anti pattern" ([49854479](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49854479)).
soleveloper worries heavy "super plan" skills make effort hard to tune ([49853982](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49853982)).

Test: remove the skill for a week. Keep it only if results got worse. Short, specific instructions
(like the templates in this repo) age better.

Also cited: [49853088](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49853088).
